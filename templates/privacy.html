Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

At Emergency QR ("we", "us", "our"), we are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and share your personal information when you use our website (the "Service").

1. Who We Are

Emergency QR is a free, non-commercial service developed as a public utility. For any privacy-related questions, you can contact us at privacy@emergency-qr.local.

2. What Data We Collect and Why

We collect the following information when you create a QR code:

  • Your Name ("Owner Name"): Used to identify you as the owner of the emergency contacts on the QR code page.
  • Emergency Contacts (Name, Phone Number, and optional Label): This information is stored to be displayed to anyone who scans your QR code in an emergency.
  • Your PIN: A 4-digit code you create. This is stored as a secure, one-way hash, which means we cannot read it. It is used only to verify your identity if you want to edit your contacts later.

We do not collect any other personal data. We do not use cookies for tracking, analytics, or marketing purposes.

3. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

We process your data based on the following legal grounds:

  • Your Consent: By creating a QR code, you give us your explicit consent to store the information you provide for the purpose of providing the Emergency QR service. You can withdraw this consent at any time by contacting us to delete your QR code.
  • Legitimate Interest: The Service operates on a legitimate interest to provide a life-saving tool. The processing is necessary to achieve this purpose, is minimal, and is not overridden by your rights, as you are in full control of the data you provide and can request its deletion.

4. How Long We Keep Your Data

We keep your data for as long as your QR code exists. If you wish to have your data and QR code deleted, you can do so by contacting us at the email address provided above. We will then permanently erase your information from our database.

5. Data Sharing and Transfers

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personally identifiable information to outside parties. Your data is stored securely on servers provided by our hosting partner, Turso (a company based in the United States). The transfer of your data to the US is protected by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, ensuring an adequate level of data protection.

6. Your Rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectify any inaccurate data (you can do this yourself via the "Edit" function on your QR page).
  • Erase your data ("the right to be forgotten").
  • Withdraw your consent at any time.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY).

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at privacy@emergency-qr.local.

7. Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal data, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.
