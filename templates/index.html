How does it work?

Four simple steps to make sure your loved ones are one scan away — even when your phone isn't.

1

Register your contacts

Write down up to three emergency contacts and pick a 4-digit PIN. No account, no email, nothing else.

2

Create the QR

In one click, the system generates a unique QR that points to your contacts. Download it in seconds.

3

Print or save it

Print the QR and keep it in your wallet, on your phone case, or take a photo of it. Anywhere you can reach it fast.

4

Use it in an emergency

Anyone can scan it with their own phone — no app, no login. Your contacts appear instantly, ready to call.
